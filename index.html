Questions

Before you enquire.

Interior projects are typically scheduled four to six weeks out, and larger exterior or commercial works are often booked a season ahead. We recommend enquiring as soon as a project is under consideration.

Yes. Every project begins with a site visit followed by a fixed, itemised quotation, so there are no surprises once work begins.

Full preparation — filling, sanding, masking and priming — is always included and never treated as an optional extra.

We carry full public liability insurance and are happy to provide certification ahead of any project.
