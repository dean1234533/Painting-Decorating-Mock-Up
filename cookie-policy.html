1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow a site to recognise your device and store some information about your preferences or past actions.

4. Analytics cookies

With your consent, we use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website — for example, which pages are most visited and how visitors navigate the site. This helps us improve the site's content and usability. These cookies are only set if you select "Accept All" or enable analytics via "Manage Preferences." No analytics cookies are set until you provide consent.

5. Managing your consent

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by selecting "Manage Cookies" in the footer of this website. You can choose to accept all cookies, reject all non-essential cookies, or manage individual categories.

6. Browser controls

Most web browsers also allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically find these settings in the "options" or "preferences" menu of your browser. You can set your browser to refuse all or some cookies, or to alert you when a website sets or accesses cookies. Please note that if you disable or refuse cookies, some parts of this website may not function as intended. For more information on managing cookies via your browser, visit aboutcookies.org.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for other operational, legal or regulatory reasons. Please revisit this page periodically to stay informed.
