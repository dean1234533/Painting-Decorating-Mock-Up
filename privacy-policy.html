Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

1. Who we are

Ashcroft & Vane ("we", "us", "our") is a painting and decorating business trading from Studio 4, The Oak Yard, Paradise Row, East London, United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. For any question relating to this policy or your data, contact us at hello@ashcroftvane.co.uk or 020 7946 0192.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Contact and enquiry form data — name, email address, phone number, property type, service required and any details you provide about your project.
  • Communications — records of correspondence by email, telephone or messaging where you contact us directly.
  • Technical and usage data — IP address, browser type, device information and pages visited, collected automatically via cookies and similar technologies (see Section 5).
  • Project and quotation records — site visit notes, photographs of the property (with consent), quotations and invoices, where you proceed with a project.

3. How we use your information

We process your personal data for the following purposes, and on the following lawful bases under UK GDPR:

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotations — legitimate interests in operating our business and responding to prospective clients.
  • To perform a contract for decorating services, including scheduling, invoicing and after-care — performance of a contract.
  • To send updates about your project — performance of a contract and legitimate interests.
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it — consent, where analytics cookies are accepted.
  • To comply with legal, accounting and tax obligations — legal obligation.

4. Contact form data

When you submit our contact form, the information you provide (name, email, phone number, service required, property type and message) is sent to our enquiries mailbox and, where applicable, stored in our client management system. We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry and, where a project proceeds, to deliver our services. We do not sell or rent this information to third parties.

5. Cookies and analytics

Our website uses cookies to operate correctly and, where you consent, to understand how visitors use the site. Full details of the categories of cookies used, their purpose and duration are set out in our Cookie Policy. You can manage your preferences at any time via the "Manage Cookies" link in the footer of this website.

Where used, analytics services (such as privacy-focused visit analytics) process data on our behalf under a data processing agreement and do not use the data for their own purposes.

6. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this policy:

  • Enquiry form submissions that do not proceed to a project are retained for 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client and project records, including quotations and invoices, are retained for 7 years to meet our accounting and tax obligations.
  • Cookie consent preferences are retained for 12 months, after which you will be asked to confirm your preferences again.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure of your data, where there is no legal reason for us to continue processing it.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@ashcroftvane.co.uk. We will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Third-party services

We may share limited data with trusted third parties strictly for the purposes of running our business, including:

  • Email and office software providers, for managing correspondence.
  • Accounting software providers, for invoicing and bookkeeping.
  • Website hosting and analytics providers, for operating and improving this website.

We do not transfer personal data outside the UK or European Economic Area except where a service provider offers appropriate safeguards in line with UK GDPR.

9. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction, including access controls, encrypted transmission and regular review of our data handling practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
